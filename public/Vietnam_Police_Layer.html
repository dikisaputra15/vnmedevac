Ministry of Public Security Central Command
Police General
Colonel General
  • Formulate and implement national security policies.
  • Direct national strategies for political security, public order, and safety.
  • Oversee intelligence, counterintelligence, investigation operations.
  • Supervise all Public Security Departments units.
Provincial Public Security Department Provincial Command
Lieutenant General
Major General
Senior Colonel
  • Maintain political security, public order, and social safety in Provincial level.
  • Prevent, detect, and investigate criminal offenses and national security threats.
  • Coordinate public security matters with other provincial government agencies.
  • Supervise public security services including the issuance of citizen identification, residence registration.
District Public Security Station District Command
Senior Colonel
Colonel
Lieutenant Colonel
  • Manage daily law enforcement, local traffic management, and crime detection.
  • Coordinate handling of administrative violations such as issue fines, confiscate illegal property, and impose administrative detentions.
  • Coordinate with District People’s Committee Chairman on local public order and business licensing.
Ward Police Station/ Commune Police Station Commune/Ward Level
Police Major
Police Captain
  • Manages the population database, national ID records, civil residency registration, temporary residence permits.
  • Coordinate crime scene preservation, record initial testimonies, perform emergency detentions, and transfer suspects to District investigators.
  • Coordinate neighborhood dispute resolution, curbing petty theft, enforcing sidewalk/traffic compliance.
  • Coordinates community-based security initiatives.
